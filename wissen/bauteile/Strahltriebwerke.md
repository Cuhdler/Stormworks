## Jet Combustion Chamber (`jet_engine_combustion_chamber`)

*A combustion chamber that burns jet fuel to sustain a jet engine.*
This is part of a set of modular jet engine components. Combustion will begin when the internal pressure is high enough.

- Größe 3x3x3 Blöcke (voxel [-1, -1, -1] .. [1, 1, 1]), Masse 20, Preis 200
- Werte: cable_length=-431602080, jet_engine_component_type=2, type=34

| Anschluss | Art | Richtung | Lage (x,y,z) | Beschreibung |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fuel | Fluessigkeit/Gas | Eingang | (0, 0, -1) | Jet fuel input required for combustion. |
| Throttle | Zahl | Eingang | (0, -1, 0) | Throttle control between 0 and 1. |
| Air Pressure | Zahl | Ausgang | (-1, 1, 0) | The pressure of air transferred by the chamber. |
| Temperature | Zahl | Ausgang | (0, 1, 0) | The temperature of air transferred by the chamber. |
| RPS | Zahl | Ausgang | (1, 1, 0) | The rotations per second generated by the chamber. |

## Jet Exhaust Afterburner (`jet_engine_exhaust_afterburner`)

This is part of a set of modular jet engine components. Thrust will be produced at the back of the exhaust proportional to the internal RPS of the engine. Additional thrust is produced when the afterburner is active, at the cost of increased fuel consumption.

## Jet Exhaust Rotating (`jet_engine_exhaust_rotating`)

*A jet engine exhaust that has a built-in mechanical pivot.*
This is part of a set of modular jet engine components. Thrust will be produced at the back of the exhaust proportional to the internal RPS of the engine. The direction of the thrust is controlled using a standard value that sets the target orientation within the pivot's range of motion of -0.5 to 0.5 turns.

- Größe 3x4x3 Blöcke (voxel [-1, 0, -1] .. [1, 3, 1]), Masse 10, Preis 800
- Werte: cable_length=-431602080, jet_engine_component_type=4, type=34

| Rotation Target | Zahl | Eingang | (0, 0, 0) | The standard value setting the orientation within the range of motion. |
